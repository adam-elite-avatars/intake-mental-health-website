## Editorial decisions

- Copy was rewritten to be warm, concise, and visitor-focused. No testimonials, credentials, success statistics, guarantees, or insurance logos were invented.
- The FAQ is newly organized from verified business information. The original published source does not provide a dedicated FAQ or a first-session protocol. The site asks visitors to contact Sandy about what to expect rather than inventing a session duration or clinical procedure.
- Telehealth is described with location eligibility to be confirmed. A homepage mention of Florida telehealth alone was not treated as proof of a specific license or nationwide service eligibility.
- The primary services are anxiety, depression, trauma/PTSD, mood disorders, support for children/adolescents, and grief/relationship/work stress/life transitions. Additional supported concerns appear in a brief note.
- Insurance names are displayed as text, using the source’s published names. Plan participation and benefits require confirmation. “Out of Network” reflects the source listing and is not an insurer.
- Fee ranges are clearly labeled **telehealth** and **sliding scale**, reflecting the telehealth source page. No general in-person price was inferred.
- The requested four-step process describes a general path to care. It does not promise a fixed protocol or treatment outcome.
- Appointment actions go to direct contact. No provider booking URL was verified, so no unverified scheduling integration is included.
- Legal notices were not supplied or verified. No placeholder privacy-policy or HIPAA links appear.
