A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Questions are
welcome here.

Getting started is easier when you know where to turn.

Ask Sandy a question
Who do you provide counseling for?

Sandy works with children, adolescents, and adults of diverse backgrounds. Counseling is tailored to each individual’s needs.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Both in-person and virtual counseling are available. Contact the practice to confirm telehealth eligibility and availability for your location.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Call 516-234-6938 or email sbakercounseling@healthintakes.com to request an appointment and discuss the next steps.

How can I prepare to get started?

You can contact Sandy to discuss what brings you to counseling and ask what to expect at your first appointment. Please keep detailed health information out of ordinary email.

Do you accept insurance?

The practice lists several accepted plans, including Aetna, Cigna, BlueCross and BlueShield, and United Healthcare, along with out-of-network options. Ask about your specific plan and benefits before scheduling.

Where are you located?

Intake Mental Health Counseling PLLC is located at 70 Glen St, Glen Cove, NY 11542.

What concerns can counseling address?

Sandy provides support for anxiety, depression, trauma and PTSD, mood disorders, grief, relationship concerns, stress, life transitions, and behavioral concerns. Reach out to discuss your needs.
